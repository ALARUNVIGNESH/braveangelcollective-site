The game

THE LIGHTS ARE ON.
SOMEONE IS HOME.
SOMEONE IS WATCHING.

Latitude of the Cult is a slow-burn siege story set over one winter night. You play Kane Marlowe — newly sworn in, alone in a cabin full of Christmas lights, waiting for someone who hasn't come home.

The town has its legends: a cult that once owned the land, something that walks the woods on two feet, a song under the lake ice. Every local tells it differently. Tonight, you decide which stories to believe — and which door to open.

ONE NIGHT, ONE CABIN

Hold the house until morning. Kill the lights. Climb to the roof. Count every round.

TWO KNOCKS

Two strangers walk in out of the storm, one after the other. Their stories almost match.

A CASE NO ONE CLOSES

The official record and what really happened are two different stories. Find the clues. Argue about the ending.

Brave Angel Collective is an independent game studio based in Chennai, India. We make story-first horror games — tight spaces, real atmosphere, and mysteries players keep talking about after the credits.

The studio was founded by Arunv, a pipeline technical director with years of Unity, Unreal and tools work behind him. Latitude of the Cult is our first release.
